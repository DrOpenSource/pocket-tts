Tip for Hindi & Hinglish (Roman vs. Devanagari)

For the smoothest pronunciation and zero glitches at word transitions, write English loanwords in Devanagari (e.g., "मीटिंग" instead of "meeting", "लिंक" instead of "link").

Why does mixed script struggle? When switching scripts mid-sentence (Devanagari ⇄ Latin), the SentencePiece tokenizer breaks BPE prefixes at script boundaries, causing token fragmentation. In addition, the flow-matching acoustic head has to abruptly jump formant distributions.

3 Recommendations:

  • Best: Transliterate loanwords to Devanagari ("कल की मीटिंग तीन बजे है, और अपडेटेड लिंक भेज दिया है"). The model pronounces English words with natural Indian phonetics!
  • If using Latin: Ensure clear spaces and add commas before English clauses ("कल की meeting, तीन बजे है") to let the acoustic state reset.
  • Spell numbers as words: Write numbers as words (e.g. "तीन बजे" instead of "3 बजे").
